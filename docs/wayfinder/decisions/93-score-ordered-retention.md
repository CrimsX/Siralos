# 93 — The Score-Ordered Retention

Governing plan 68 · entry review [79](79-context-management-foundations.md) · Map.

> **User-directed 2026-08-31 (session HITL).** The externally-discussed resolution of the decision 92 re-gate: search score feeds ONLY the assembly demotion order (rank-for-retention, not rank-for-depth — any score-decides-depth mechanism re-measures the decision 88 rejection and is refused); the benchmark flow is frozen and carries no GO claim; adoption is gated on the pre-committed byte-identity and determinism branches.

## 2. The mechanism (verbatim) + concretization + adoption branches

**Mechanism (signed, verbatim):**

> "When assembly-budget enforcement must demote HOT nodes (after the pinned 1024 pinned-HOT quota, then the 4096 budget, unique-digest), retain nodes in decreasing decision-92 search-score order with node_id-ascending tiebreak, demoting lowest score first, and never assemble any node beyond its L1 summary on the basis of score."

**Concretization (pre-committed):**

TickInput gains an OPTIONAL bounded latest-observed-search-scores field — at most 64 entries, canonical form node_id ascending, one entry per node, sourced ONLY from host-observed context.search results (the integer score decision 92 already emits per hit), delivered through the B3 composition path exactly like events; the model can never inject scores. When the field is present and non-empty, assembly-budget demotion orders by: lower retained search score first; nodes absent from the scores map are treated as score 0; ties break by the existing scheduler-score order, then node_id ascending. When the field is absent, the demotion order is UNCHANGED (existing scheduler-score order). The pin-quota demotion order (decision 89) is UNCHANGED by this mechanism — search-score ordering applies ONLY to the general budget demotion in assembly.

**Pre-committed adoption branches (verbatim):**

> "Adopt the mechanism into the pinned assembly semantics IFF (a) the context-benchmark record is byte-identical to v69 (the decision 88 Retained outcome stands verbatim) AND (b) the new demotion-order tests pass deterministically (byte-equal canonical JSON) AND (c) stub/neighbor caps and budget accounting (decision 90 B2) are unchanged. Else decision 92's verdicts stand in full (87-GO-does-not-stand, 88-Retained-stands), the mechanism is rejected, and the assembly demotion order stays canonical."

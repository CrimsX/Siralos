## Summary

HITL confirmed the **Stage 4.6 QA Workflows slice** with the bounded-workflow-steps framing: the fourth consumer of the same identity-bound primitive gate. `siralos-core::runtime` gains a bounded QA workflow model (ordered typed steps composed over the generic runtime boundary) routed through the existing generic decision order over the unchanged 13-kind taxonomy and 6-disposition table; a workflow with zero steps is a typed pairing refusal, mirroring the one-shot-interaction and headless-capture refusals. The QA-workflow execution primitive is identity-bound and absent on this platform, so every otherwise-valid workflow reports typed `UNAVAILABLE`. The task-kernel-acceptance alternative was considered and **rejected as already landed** (AcceptanceEvaluator + completion gate, Stage 3). Because corpus v36 reached the 256-scenario cap exactly, HITL also approved a **deliberate candidate-side cap raise `256 → 384`** in both `contract.mjs` `CONTRACT_LIMITS.scenarios` and `harness.rs` `MAX_SCENARIOS` — the pinned v32 oracle and freeze evidence are untouched. One frozen differential subject `qa-workflow` ×4 at corpus v37; nothing flips `unavailable`.

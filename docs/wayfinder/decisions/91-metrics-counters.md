# 91 — The Metrics Counters

Governing plan 68 · entry review [79](79-context-management-foundations.md) · Map.

> **User-directed 2026-08-31 (session HITL).** The audit and hygiene-metrics seam is implemented as pure in-memory observability, un-gated by the decision 87 corrected-baseline GO: saturating u64 counters and a capped 64-record tick ring, all derived deterministically from tick inputs and outputs; the scheduler tick stays pure, nothing persists, and live activation remains precondition-gated per decision 84.
